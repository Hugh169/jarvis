JARVIS — heads-up display

The HUD is the whole interface: a non-activating panel pinned top-centre, growing downward as a turn unfolds. It shows what was heard, which apps are being touched right now, and the spoken reply. Press Run a turn to watch a full one.

JARVIS

Two channels

The voice says one or two sentences. Anything structured goes to the pane below it, so the spoken reply never reads a table aloud.

Real app icons

Each chip carries the icon of the app actually being driven, pulled from NSWorkspace at runtime. Glanceable proof of what it touched.

Mono means machine

Speech is set in SF Pro, tool names and timings in SF Mono. The typeface tells you whether a human or the machine produced that line.

Always dark

The HUD commits to one dark treatment because it floats over arbitrary desktops. This page around it follows your system theme.
